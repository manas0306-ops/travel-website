Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about planning trips with VoyageX.

How are travel budgets estimated on VoyageX? +
Our budget calculator uses curated empirical daily cost baselines for each destination across accommodation, food, local transit, and activities. The algorithm scales costs dynamically based on your chosen travel style (Budget, Standard, or Luxury) and party size.
Do I need to create an account to save trips or favorites? +
No account or login is required! VoyageX is client-first and privacy-respecting. All your saved favorite destinations, generated itineraries, and packing checklist progress are saved locally in your browser's persistent localStorage.
Can I customize the generated itineraries? +
Absolutely. You can modify trip duration from 1 to 14 days, toggle interests (such as food, nature, culture, photography, or adventure), and click "Regenerate Itinerary" to generate fresh schedules. You can also print or export the plan to PDF.
Is VoyageX free to use? +
Yes, 100% free! VoyageX is built as an open, modern travel technology showcase. There are no paywalls, hidden fees, or subscription charges.
